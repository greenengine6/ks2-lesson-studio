Every engine makes a class screen to share and teacher notes to keep. Each one comes with a finished example, so you can teach with it straight away.

What pupils see

Two-way screens, not worksheets.

  • Pupils answer all the time. Chat waterfalls, live votes, code locks and a robot to program.
  • Answers stay hidden until you tap, so you can share your screen.
  • A magic pointer shows pupils on Teams or Zoom exactly where you are pointing.
  • Teacher notes with timings, answers and support ideas, ready to copy.

Make it yours

Switch on what you need.

Each switch lights up and remembers your choice on this device.

Teaching online

How it works

Ready for your next online class.

1

Pick an engine

From the dock at the bottom of the screen, or the cards above.

2

Fill in two or three boxes

Year group, topic and one choice. Symphony writes it in about a minute.

3

Press Present

Share that window on Teams, Zoom or Meet. Tick “share sound” for Sound Journey and Brain Break.
