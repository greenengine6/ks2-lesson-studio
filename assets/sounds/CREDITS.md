# Sounds: where they come from

Every sound file here is either a field recording or instrument sample with a clear free licence, or was designed
for this site (and is free to reuse under the site's own terms). The site never needs these files to work: until a
file has arrived (or offline) it plays its older live-made sound instead.

## Recorded instruments (`i-*.mp3`, the music's notes, and the notes in `ui.mp3`, `fx-chime`, `fx-sparkle`, `fx-land`, `fx-glow`, `fx-petals`, `fx-lantern`, `fx-candle`, `fx-bell`, `fx-drum`, `fx-tick`, `fx-cheer`, `drums.mp3`)

Notes from the **FluidR3 GM** soundfont by Frank Wen and contributors, released under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/), as rendered by
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts): koto, steel- and nylon-string guitar,
banjo, sitar, dulcimer, harp, vibraphone, glockenspiel, celesta, tubular bells, marimba, xylophone, clarinet, pan
flute, ocarina, taiko drum, melodic tom, timpani, woodblock, agogo and applause.

## Designed for this site

Made offline with the site's sound build (numpy and scipy: modal synthesis for bells, gongs and drums, physically
based bubbles and drops, granular textures, a voice model with formants for animal calls, convolution reverb):
the cicada chorus (`bed-cicadas.mp3`), the cave's drips (`bed-drips.mp3`), the frog chorus (`bed-frogs.mp3`,
`fx-frogs.mp3`), the calm pad (`pad.mp3`), and the effects: splash, pop, thunder (with the storm recording under
it), flare, candle, fireworks, firecrackers, gong, cheer (with the applause recording), hooves, jet plane, swell,
puff of colour, rattle, tiger's roar, elephant's trumpet, rhinoceros hornbill, proboscis monkeys, orangutan's long
call, bats, fireflies' glow, rustling leaves, turtle hatchlings, wingbeats, and the soft taps, whooshes and paper
sounds in `ui.mp3` and `drums.mp3`.
